Betta surface audit — 2026-10-21
Read-only comparison of cloned AquaPearlRosetail in Unity Editor.
No production scripts, meshes, materials, prefab, or scene were changed.

Four camera views, Idle and Swim poses, five conditions (40 captures):
full: current material and property block.
no-normal: current material, normal map disabled.
plain: uniform diffuse gray, no texture maps, metallic, smoothness, specular highlights or environment reflections.
recomputed: plain material with opaque-skin face normals averaged at coincident positions; identical vertex positions.
unlit: uniform unlit silhouette.
Fins and BODY membrane submesh hidden to expose body surface. Eyes retained.
Lighting and camera held constant across conditions for each pose/view.
All temporary clones, lights, materials and meshes destroyed; original scene light masks restored.

Findings:
1. Removing normal texture reduces the crinkled scale appearance. Remaining source color/shading and glossy response also emphasize unevenness.
2. Large cheek/head planes, belly transitions and a ridge toward the tail remain on diffuse gray; they are not caused solely by color or normal texture.
3. Recomputed normals alter some local shading but do not remove the main broad planes.
4. Both Idle and Swim show these features. Current swim deformation is not sufficient to explain them.
5. Current body profile remaps section height/width but largely retains original section irregularities. More width alone does not ensure smooth cross-sectional curvature.
6. Most middle-body sections are already close to elliptical; head/gill anatomy naturally departs from an ellipse. Do not circularize the entire fish or treat every concavity as an error.

Limits: Editor comparison for one active color variant; no device build was made. Recomputed normals are diagnostic only, not an anatomical reference. Multiple skin sheets, seams, and intended creases can affect normal/section metrics.
